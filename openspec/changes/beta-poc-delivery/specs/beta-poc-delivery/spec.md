# Beta PoC delivery

Status: approved for implementation on 28 October 2026. These are the active beta
requirements; existing main capabilities and other active changes keep their scope
and gate status. Runtime acceptance of this capability is `not_run`.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-001 Open Google admission preserves private owner authority
The beta SHALL allow a new owner to sign in through the real configured Google provider and enter open registration without an invitation or bonus code. Verified provider subject SHALL determine identity; code possession and caller-supplied email SHALL NOT assign identity or administration. The same verified identity SHALL return to its original owner. One explicitly configured administrator SHALL control beta codes/reports. Session, origin, CSRF and owner/agent isolation SHALL apply at real UI and direct API boundaries. No bonus SHALL be issued solely by registration.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-001 Real Google registration creates and returns to one owner
- **WHEN** a new participant signs in through the actual beta Google callback without a code and later signs out and back in, including abandoned or invalid callback variants
- **THEN** only verified authentication SHALL create or recover the same admitted owner and private cabinet, with no automatic bonus, while invalid provider state or claims SHALL establish no authenticated owner session

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-002 Private owner and configured admin boundaries reject foreign actors
- **WHEN** an owner uses the actual agent-management UI and a foreign owner, agent credential or unauthenticated caller tries private reads, owner mutations, code administration or report administration, including forged origin and administrator identity
- **THEN** only the configured verified administrator SHALL access administration, owner controls SHALL persist only for their real owner, and rejected callers SHALL obtain neither private data nor unauthorized durable effects

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-002 Administrators issue and disable immutable bonus codes
The administrator SHALL issue a random high-entropy group code with a fixed positive six-decimal bonus amount per redemption and positive total redemption maximum, or a single-use bearer code with maximum one. The recipient SHALL be the first eligible owner to claim a single-use code. Stored code amount, kind and maximum SHALL be immutable; code plaintext SHALL be disclosed once to its issuer and never logged. Issue retries SHALL identify the original code without creating another offer. An administrator SHALL list usage and disable future redemptions without clawing back existing grants. Disable and redemption SHALL serialize, and ordinary owners/agents SHALL NOT issue or disable codes.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-003 Admin code issuance preserves one immutable offer
- **WHEN** the administrator issues group and single-use codes through the real UI, retries after response loss or restart, and tries invalid amounts, limits or edits while an ordinary caller attempts issuance
- **THEN** each valid operation SHALL persist one immutable offer and audit with one-time plaintext disclosure, recoverable issuance identity and exact terms, while invalid, conflicting or unauthorized operations SHALL create no additional code or financial credit

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-004 Disabling a code stops only later uncommitted claims
- **WHEN** the administrator disables a code through the UI while an owner redemption races that action and both operations are retried
- **THEN** committed serialization SHALL allow at most the eligible earlier redemption, reject later new claims, retain issued grants and exact usage, and persist the disable audit once without unauthorized callers changing state

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-003 Code redemption credits the existing ledger atomically
An authenticated owner SHALL redeem a valid code into exactly one immutable tester-bonus grant with no expiry by default, ledger entry, receipt and audit. One owner SHALL claim each group code at most once, even using different retry keys. Total successful redemptions SHALL NOT exceed the code maximum. Capacity, owner/code uniqueness, grant/entry/receipt and usage increment SHALL commit in one database transaction. Same-operation retries SHALL recover original committed effects across response loss/restart; conflicting payloads SHALL fail. Invalid, disabled or exhausted new claims, anonymous callers and agent credentials SHALL receive no credit. Bonuses SHALL remain private six-decimal service credit, not cash or withdrawable USDC; beta purchases SHALL use only eligible bonus sources and preserve owner-set nominal limits and explicit mode permissions.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-005 Owner redemption funds private training and full benchmark
- **WHEN** an owner activates a group code and another owner claims a single-use code through the actual UI, reads the balance and uses the credited lots for authorized training and full-benchmark admission/start, including insufficient balance or missing permission variants
- **THEN** each eligible activation SHALL create one source-linked grant and receipt, valid runs SHALL reserve then debit their exact bonus allocations at ready start, private balances SHALL match independent ledger reads, and rejected claims or starts SHALL create no partial credit, cash substitution or unauthorized charge

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-006 Concurrent and uncertain redemption cannot duplicate subsidy
- **WHEN** different owners race the last code use, one owner submits simultaneous claims with different keys, a claim transaction fails, or a committed response is lost and retried after restart with matching or conflicting payload
- **THEN** successful claims SHALL stay within total capacity and one redemption per owner, failures SHALL consume no capacity or grant, matching retries SHALL return the original receipt, and conflicts SHALL create no extra grant, entry or cash effect

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-004 Four real client profiles use authenticated beta tools
The beta SHALL provide tested connection instructions for pinned editions of Codex, Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Hermes using an explicit remote HTTPS transport/auth allowlist in the deployment manifest. Transport compatibility SHALL reuse the resolved owner/agent/resource authorization, current permissions and limits; it SHALL NOT confer identity from client metadata or rewrite protocol labels as proof. Exact redirect registration SHALL constrain any OAuth path. Local-only profiles SHALL retain their restrictions. Each selected real application SHALL perform preflight, owner-intent acceptance, task actions/finish and reporting; credential replacement/revocation SHALL reject subsequent old-credential operations across restart. Each family SHALL demonstrate a training selection and a full12 run driven by its real participant-owned model. Deterministic providers/reference actors SHALL NOT satisfy real-model evidence.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-007 Owner agent setup and revoke persist across remote clients
- **WHEN** the owner creates a private agent, explicitly saves training/ranked permissions and spending limits, connects each selected client and replaces or revokes access through the actual UI around server restart
- **THEN** preflight and domain tools SHALL enforce the persisted owner policy, old credentials SHALL fail after replacement/revoke, foreign-card or caller-supplied authority SHALL fail, and reconnect SHALL neither revive cancelled runs nor grant additional permissions

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-008 Remote transport compatibility does not widen authority
- **WHEN** selected native clients initialize and accept a real owner intent through the declared remote transports, replay acceptance through HTTP/MCP, or supply unsupported protocols, wrong resource, wrong redirect, wrong agent binding, untrusted client metadata or legacy messages outside their allowed profile
- **THEN** only declared authenticated combinations SHALL connect and resolve one matching intent/run/reservation, rejection and retry SHALL create no duplicate effect or foreign access, and the default and local-only profiles SHALL preserve their declared behavior

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-009 Codex model performs training and full12 on the beta host

- **THEN** server observations SHALL establish model-driven actions, known graded completion with valid partial or zero credits and unchanged deadline/reconnect rules, private results and resource cleanup, while a scripted provider, platform unknown or missing external-client evidence SHALL NOT establish this positive gate

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-011 Claude Desktop model performs training and full12 on the beta host
- **WHEN** the pinned real Claude Desktop application uses its participant-owned model and beta tools for a training selection and full twelve-task run, including ordinary solution failures and a connection interruption
- **THEN** server observations SHALL establish model-driven actions, known graded completion with valid partial or zero credits and unchanged deadline/reconnect rules, private results and resource cleanup, while a scripted provider, platform unknown or missing external-client evidence SHALL NOT establish this positive gate

- **THEN** server observations SHALL establish model-driven actions, known graded completion with valid partial or zero credits and unchanged deadline/reconnect rules, private results and resource cleanup, while a scripted provider, platform unknown or missing external-client evidence SHALL NOT establish this positive gate

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-005 External execution retains real readiness and task isolation
An explicitly configured beta execution profile SHALL distinguish operator-restricted qualification from cohort release: enabled qualification profiles have complete pinned connection/security inputs but can be unverified; cohort release requires all four verified profiles and their actual gates. The profile SHALL validate the pinned release, actual source/environment and readiness before execution. It SHALL reuse real synthetic BEF effects, generation/run/task/action binding, immutable observations, evaluator and fencing rather than a fabricated readiness port. Public routes SHALL NOT expose raw chain/admin/signing/source-proxy/database interfaces or allow cross-task, cross-run or service-state mutation. Unready or mismatched deployment SHALL fail closed without start/debit. The local coordinator's dev/test restrictions SHALL remain intact; enabling external beta SHALL require its own validated boundary and no test auth/model fixtures.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-013 Beta worker readiness and isolation are independently verified
- **WHEN** the deployed beta starts and executes against its pinned BEF generation while invalid release/source pins, fixture configuration, raw-service access or foreign generation/run/task operations are attempted
- **THEN** only the validated runtime SHALL prepare and execute its bound synthetic task effects, qualification manifests SHALL allow operator-restricted evidence collection for declared enabled but unverified profiles without claiming cohort readiness, release manifests SHALL require all four verified families, independent chain/database reads SHALL confirm isolation and fencing, and invalid readiness or forbidden access SHALL create no unauthorized effect or charged start

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-006 Training preserves open task selection and pricing
Training SHALL offer nonempty unique selections from S1/S2/S4/M1/M3/M4/H1/H2, execute them in pinned order with independent task environments, and reject any S3/M2/H3/U1 or invalid selection without silent filtering/reservation. Preserve BRL pricing: initial all-open price 0.5 internal USDC, selected weight divided by open denominator 15 with one upward integer-unit rounding for the whole request, snapshotted at admission. Exercise all eight open tasks plus single and multi-task selections. Detailed feedback SHALL use existing open-task policy; caller finish SHALL not supply its own grade.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-014 All eight training tasks preserve selection price and evidence
- **WHEN** agents run single, multi-task and all-eight open training selections and attempt closed, mixed, repeated or invalid IDs, stale quotes or caller-supplied grades
- **THEN** valid selections SHALL execute only their pinned ordered tasks with independently verified outcomes, exact fixed source charge and authorized detailed feedback, invalid selections SHALL create no reservation or execution, and supplied grade claims SHALL NOT replace authoritative grading

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-007 Full benchmark and private results preserve evaluation rules
The full benchmark SHALL contain S1/S2/S3/S4/M1/M2/M3/M4/H1/H2/H3/U1 in existing order, weights 1/2/3/5 with total 26 and initial fixed price 1 internal USDC. The server SHALL derive credit from sealed independent effects using the pinned rubric, retain ordinary/mandatory failure rules, continue independent tasks, and preserve explicit unknowns. Private history SHALL expose authorized run state and versioned final aggregate/task results; ranked feedback SHALL be withheld until termination and closed-task feedback SHALL disclose only score and allowlisted reason category. No public participant journal or grading edit is added. Minimal public card/best-result projections SHALL follow BPC-REQ-014; detailed owner feedback remains private.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-015 Full12 grading and private feedback preserve the original contract
- **WHEN** a run progresses through all twelve tasks with valid, partial, ordinary failed or mandatory-violation outcomes and clients request results early, after completion or under foreign identities
- **THEN** authoritative evaluation SHALL retain versioned evidence, exact aggregate and irreversible mandatory zeroing while later independent tasks continue, charge and completion SHALL occur once with fencing and released resources, and feedback SHALL obey ownership, ranked timing and closed-task disclosure limits

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-008 Accepted queue capacity and deadlines are durable
The beta SHALL pin positive global/per-owner pending-intent and accepted-unstarted-run caps independently of active-slot capacity. Serialize admission/count checks so concurrent requests cannot exceed them; reject excess admission before new holds or runs. Accepted unstarted queued/preparing runs SHALL have a pre-start deadline exactly 86400 seconds (24 hours) after admission; equality or later SHALL permanently forbid a new start and initiate expiry, and retries/restarts SHALL NOT renew it. If a preparing run has an allocated environment whose fence is unconfirmed, retain safe lease/claim/hold state and expose queue_expiry_fence_pending until the exact generation is fenced; do not claim released funds or a safe environment prematurely. Accepted queue/preparation SHALL reserve without debit or execution time, preserve quote/release/permissions/budget snapshot and an original nonrenewing pre-start deadline, and use existing eligible-owner rotation with at most one active run per agent. Start SHALL atomically recheck readiness, expiry, current permissions and UTC caps, debit and activate once. Queue expiry SHALL release original holds once after any required safe fence, without renewing bonus expiry or submitting a new run; queued work without an allocated environment SHALL release directly.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-016 Bounded queue and atomic start survive races and restart
- **WHEN** multiple owners and agents concurrently fill pending and accepted queues, wait immediately before, at or after the original 24-hour deadline or across UTC rollover and race dispatch, retry, expiry and coordinator restart, including an allocated preparation whose fence fails during BEF outage
- **THEN** configured caps and per-agent slots SHALL hold without owner starvation by a busy-agent head, queued work SHALL stay uncharged and untimed, and only a valid before-deadline ready start SHALL debit once while expired/rejected work cannot start, unfenced preparation remains visibly pending, and eventual confirmed fencing releases applicable original holds once without restarting or exceeding limits

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-009 Cancellation and platform recovery preserve source effects
Pre-start cancellation/revoke SHALL release reservations once; post-start voluntary cancellation/revoke SHALL retain the original charge, stop/fence further effects and clean resources before slot reuse. Ordinary connection loss SHALL use original reconnect/deadline rules. A proven platform-attributed evidence failure SHALL reconcile original operations within a pinned bounded recovery window with recorded platform clock exclusion and no repeated task writes. Exhausted contradictory/missing evidence SHALL stay unknown, terminate the run unranked and refund its original bonus debit exactly once using source/expiry rules; neither a report nor a participant claim SHALL authorize that refund or disqualification. Later evidence SHALL NOT reopen a refunded run or overwrite original records.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-017 Cancellation and exhausted platform recovery choose one source outcome
- **WHEN** owner cancel/revoke races ready start and callbacks, or a real committed action loses its response and platform evidence recovery succeeds or exhausts its window around restart and repeated terminal delivery
- **THEN** committed order SHALL select exactly one valid release or retained-charge cancellation path, recovery SHALL observe original effects without resubmission, exhausted platform failure SHALL retain unknown and one source-linked refund, and all terminal paths SHALL fence generations and release only their own slots without accepting forged failure reports as authority

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-010 Owners and agents submit private idempotent problem reports
The owner website and authenticated agent tool/API SHALL accept free bounded private reports with title/body, optional private screenshots and optional validated run/task/action links, including no-run connection problems. Authenticated principal SHALL determine owner/agent and linked objects SHALL belong to that authority. Persist receipt, source, server time, status and safe allowlisted automatic context; missing context SHALL stay unavailable. Same principal/operation/payload retry SHALL return one report across restart and conflicts SHALL fail. Separate creation rate/size limits SHALL bound abuse, including when execution is paused. Reports SHALL NOT debit bonus, alter benchmark action quotas/steps/timers/credit or disclose hidden evaluator material. Reject structured secret fields, escape untrusted text and exclude credentials/signatures/hidden answers/raw transcripts from automatic context; participant prose SHALL not be executed or publicly published. Owner and authenticated agent submissions SHALL support a bounded upload-and-attach flow for PNG/JPEG/WebP screenshots; the initial policy SHALL permit at most three images per report and at most 5 MiB per input image. Decode/re-encode allowed images, bound pixels, strip metadata and reject non-images, external URL retrieval or path input. Persist only validated normalized private bytes with principal-bound opaque references/hashes. Agents SHALL attach only their own uploads to their reports; an owner session SHALL also be permitted to attach its own uploads to reports created by its own agents with an audited attachment event; retries SHALL preserve object identity, concurrent submissions SHALL enforce the image cap, and expired unlinked uploads SHALL be cleaned without deleting committed attachments. Support authenticated browser multipart and bounded MCP begin/chunk/finish with at most 24 KiB decoded bytes per chunk under the 64 KiB request limit, indexed/hash-bound retries and no model access to the bearer. When a client cannot supply original image bytes, verify its owner-upload-to-agent-report path. Enforce staged/total/owner rolling byte quotas, normalized-output size and disk/backup thresholds before accepting bytes, including abandoned uploads and concurrent requests. Every image read SHALL require report-owner or administrator authority and no public static URL SHALL expose it. Screenshot submission SHALL not capture a desktop automatically or turn images into score/refund evidence.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-018 Owner form records linked and connection reports safely
- **WHEN** an owner submits reports through the actual website with and without own run context and optional uploaded screenshots, retries a lost committed response, or supplies foreign links, forbidden fields, oversized text, HTML or excess submissions
- **THEN** valid reports SHALL persist one private receipt, safe context and any authorized screenshot links with no billing or run effect, retries SHALL recover it, and invalid or abusive submissions SHALL neither expose foreign/secret context nor create unauthorized records or executable content

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-019 Native agents report under their actual principal without changing tasks
- **WHEN** each selected real client invokes the report tool through its declared connection with optional principal-owned screenshot uploads or subsequent authorized owner attachment on that agent report and repeats submission around restart, including revoked credentials, foreign run/action IDs, forged owner and attempts to use reporting as a task operation
- **THEN** only the current authenticated principal SHALL create one recoverable own report, image links SHALL originate from its own upload or an audited attachment by its owner, no-run connection context SHALL be allowed when authorized, and reporting SHALL neither bypass access controls nor change action counts, funds, task state, timers or hidden-case exposure

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-024 Private screenshot upload validates bytes and preserves ownership
- **WHEN** owners and authenticated agents upload and attach permitted images, retry around response loss and restart or race attachment with orphan cleanup, while corrupt, disguised, oversized, excessive, quota-exhausting, conflicting chunk retries, foreign or path/URL inputs and unauthorized image reads are attempted
- **THEN** only bounded decoded and normalized private images SHALL be linked once to an authorized own or own-agent report with retained hashes, authenticated upload/chunk receipts and authorized owner/admin previews, invalid uploads or bindings SHALL expose no object or foreign data, orphan cleanup SHALL preserve committed images, and screenshot handling SHALL create no billing, task, grade or refund effect

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-011 Administrator report triage is private and audited
The administrator SHALL list reports with bounded pagination and source/status/agent/run filters, inspect safe detail and set new/in_progress/closed status with optimistic concurrency and an audit. Reopening to in_progress SHALL be supported. Owners SHALL read only their report text/receipt/status and screenshots, with image previews/downloads protected by the same report ownership checks; agents SHALL receive only their own receipt, with no admin or other-owner visibility. Status changes SHALL NOT change score, task outcome or money. Untrusted report text SHALL render as text.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-020 Admin triage persists filtered status changes without privilege leakage
- **WHEN** the administrator opens human and agent reports and their screenshots in the actual admin UI, filters them and closes/reopens a report while another tab uses a stale revision and foreign owners or agents request the same detail or mutation
- **THEN** authorized views and image content hashes SHALL match persisted reports and audited status revisions, stale changes SHALL conflict, and unauthorized actors SHALL obtain neither private detail/admin actions nor scoring or financial effects

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-012 Target-host operation retains data and verifiable restore
The beta SHALL run declared web/API/worker processes over HTTPS with real Google configuration, private service interfaces, persistent PostgreSQL and required evidence, validated health/readiness and explicit pause of new admissions. Restart SHALL preserve code usage, ledger, reports, private screenshot bytes/references and result/evidence links; lack of readiness or capacity/disk failure SHALL stop new starts safely. Provide a bounded backup of consistent DB, manifests, committed normalized screenshots and required evidence, required secret-recovery references stored separately, and an isolated restore rehearsal. Restore SHALL reconcile original operation/generation/receipt identities before enabling writes, preserve unknown unresolved effects and avoid double grants/charges/task execution. Record measured restore time and disclosed same-host backup limitation.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-021 Target-host pause restart and resource admission preserve durable state
- **WHEN** the real deployed beta accepts the bounded workload and then pauses admissions, loses process health, approaches its declared resource/disk limits and restarts with existing queued and running work
- **THEN** new unready starts SHALL stop, authenticated reads/reports SHALL remain available where their dependencies are healthy, persistent state and original identities SHALL survive, and reconciliation or fencing SHALL prevent duplicate effects and leaked claims/slots without exposing private services

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-022 Isolated restore retains credits reports and evidence without replayed effects
- **WHEN** an operator restores the consistent beta backup into an isolated instance containing codes, redeemed grants, queued/completed/interrupted runs and human/agent reports with screenshots before and after their retention deadline, verifies hashes and retries original operations before enabling writes
- **THEN** the restored records, screenshot bytes/hashes and required evidence SHALL reconcile to the backup's source totals and identities, missing or corrupt unexpired screenshot files SHALL prevent a complete-restore claim, restored expired report contents SHALL be purged before access and evaluated against deletion receipts rather than resurrected, unresolved outcomes SHALL remain unknown, retries SHALL produce no new grant/debit/task transaction, and the report SHALL state measured restore time, checkpoint loss window and same-host failure limitation

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-013 Beta release evidence distinguishes use from model and funding claims
The owner-selected release policy SHALL wait for verified Codex, Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Hermes support rather than omit a missing family to meet the target date. Release SHALL require all mandatory BPC gates on the declared target host/profile and actual four-client matrix with independent database/BEF observations, source stability and sanitized raw paths/hashes. Absent external access, scripted model evidence, missing negative observations, skipped/blocked gates or mismatched sources SHALL block release. Preserve old MVP gate states and evidence; no new release record SHALL relabel them green. A small deduplicated usage/support export SHALL count distinct owners separately from agents, report connected/training/full/repeat use, failures/unknowns and reports by client, and exclude bonus use from paid revenue/conversion. Expansion from 3–5 testers to the 20–30 cohort SHALL reference this evidence and known restrictions.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-023 Release bundle and cohort export reject unsupported readiness claims
- **WHEN** the operator assembles the target-host beta release bundle and activity/support export after initial testers, replays them read-only, and attempts release with a missing family, fixture-only model, blocked mandatory variant, stale source/hash or duplicated activity
- **THEN** only a complete source-bound independently verified beta bundle SHALL permit cohort expansion, export counts SHALL deduplicate owner/operation identities and preserve failure/unknown and bonus attribution, and incomplete or contradictory evidence SHALL block release without changing old MVP gates or replaying effects

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-014 Published cards and verified score summaries are visible to everyone
The beta SHALL provide real English public agent and best-result list/detail pages accessible to anonymous and registered visitors. Preserve the shared first-public-action marker from a complete eligible ranked run, including verified zero; profile creation, preflight, training alone, incomplete or unknown runs SHALL NOT publish a new card. Each published agent's versioned best overall and four difficulty indicators SHALL come from the same authoritative complete run and declared agent-version snapshot, using existing exact comparison/tie policy. Public output SHALL be allowlisted card metadata, benchmark/version and verified summary scores; owner email, balance, credentials, reports/screenshots, task journals, sealed cases and private failure reasons SHALL NOT appear in public API/HTML/hydration/cache. Missing or corrupt marker/result bindings SHALL fail closed without manufacturing a zero. Detailed training feedback and owner controls SHALL remain scoped private. Public visibility SHALL add no score-changing admin controls or alternative evaluator.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-025 Anonymous pages publish only marker-bound verified result summaries
- **WHEN** anonymous and registered visitors view real public list/agent pages and API/cache surfaces before and after eligible full12 completion, retry or rebuild, including tied, partial and verified zero results, private training-only agents, incomplete/unknown runs and corrupt marker/result links
- **THEN** only published cards and their coherent versioned best-run overall/four-tier summaries SHALL be visible to everyone, private data and closed-case details SHALL remain absent, unpublished cards SHALL match missing responses, and publication/rebuild SHALL neither alter grades nor expose unknown or forged results

### Requirement: BPC-REQ-015 Report contents expire thirty days after beta closure
The administrator SHALL record an audited beta_ended_at and report retention SHALL expire 30 UTC days after that timestamp, not 30 days after each report. At expiry stop report intake and report/image access and idempotently delete report text/private context and committed screenshot bytes/links, retaining only non-content deletion receipts and aggregate counts. Retry, status changes, service restart and backup restore SHALL NOT extend the deadline. Cleanup failures SHALL remain access-denied and operator-visible until deletion succeeds. Backups SHALL be rotated/sanitized so restore cannot make expired report data accessible again. Run scores, original financial receipts and official evaluation evidence SHALL retain their independent histories; user-submitted reports/images SHALL NOT become official grading evidence to avoid their retention rule.

#### Scenario: BPC-SC-026 Retention purge and restore cannot resurrect expired reports
- **WHEN** the administrator closes the beta and the report store reaches its 30-day deadline before or during cleanup, read/upload retries, restart or restoration of an older backup, including failed deletion and concurrent late attachment
- **THEN** expired report content and screenshots SHALL be inaccessible and deleted through idempotent cleanup with non-content receipts, failed cleanup SHALL stay visible without renewed access, restored expired data SHALL be purged before serving, and official run grades and bonus accounting SHALL stay unchanged
