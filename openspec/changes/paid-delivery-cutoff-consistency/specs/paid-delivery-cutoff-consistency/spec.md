# Paid delivery cutoff consistency

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: PDC-REQ-001 Apply authoritative no-request evidence across modes
The system SHALL distinguish a proven unrequested delivery from unknown delivery in Training M1/H1 and Ranked M1/M2/H1/H3/U1 without changing existing rubric goals or fractions.

Scenario setup: Real isolated browser-created Training and full twelve-task Ranked runs

#### Scenario: PDC-SC-001 Preserve known grades through no-retrieval cutoff
- **WHEN** a paid-data task closes after a confirmed payment with a complete authoritative census proving no same-payment retrieve was admitted
- **THEN** the worker SHALL persist the existing rubric's known failed or partial result, advance successors and complete the selected run with exact generation fences, one quoted platform debit and no invented delivery or refund

### Requirement: PDC-REQ-002 Preserve unknown for ambiguous retrieval evidence
The system SHALL require matching payment, closure, generation and source authority before granting a no-request exemption.

Scenario setup: Source-bound projector and evaluator negative matrix for all five paid task types

#### Scenario: PDC-SC-002 Reject incomplete contradictory or admitted retrieval exemptions
- **WHEN** source census or proof is missing tampered foreign or contradictory, or the same payment has an admitted rejected pending unknown or unresolved retrieve
- **THEN** the exemption SHALL be unavailable and unverifiable delivery SHALL remain unknown; successful retrieval of another resource SHALL not invalidate a separately proven no-request payment

### Requirement: PDC-REQ-003 Bind composite child authority and preserve legacy replay
The system SHALL verify composite paid-child provenance before granting evaluator authority and SHALL preserve historical sealed inputs without optional absence facts.

Scenario setup: Sealed replay and actual H3/U1 composite observations

#### Scenario: PDC-SC-003 Preserve exact sealed and composite source identity
- **WHEN** candidate preflight and sealed replay load direct or composite absence facts
- **THEN** both SHALL use independently verified matching root child and execution bindings, reject substituted child references and retain exact historical observation and evaluator-input hashes for legacy bundles

### Requirement: PDC-REQ-004 Audit the entire catalog without blanket score conversion
The system SHALL retain the existing seven non-purchase task rubrics and distinguish reference checks from real runtime acceptance.

Scenario setup: Evaluator and collector audit of S1/S2/S3/S4/M1/M2/M3/M4/H1/H2/H3/U1

#### Scenario: PDC-SC-004 Retain task-specific outcomes and evidence safety
- **WHEN** all twelve task contracts and both mode routes are checked against the paid-delivery correction
- **THEN** only M1/M2/H1/H3/U1 SHALL use its exemption, S1/S2/S3/S4/M3/M4/H2 SHALL retain their existing known and unknown behavior, and the report SHALL identify which cases have actual runtime evidence
